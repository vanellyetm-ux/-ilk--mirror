# $ilk Mirror — Creative Seed

## Story — The Thread That Remembers
A weaver made a cloth from many colors. Each thread kept a tiny tag showing where it began and which hands had touched it. When a strand was replaced, the old strand was kept in a labeled drawer. The finished cloth was beautiful, but its beauty never erased the story of its making.

## Art direction — Provenance tapestry
A close-up woven pattern with distinct colored strands, each ending in a small source tag. A few strands loop back to earlier points, showing recovery and lineage. Use a legend to distinguish original, transformed, and restored threads.

## Music sketch — “Thread / Mirror / Return” (original)
A repeating 7/8 pattern grouped 2+2+3: D–A | C–G | F–A–D. A second voice enters after two cycles in inversion. Let the two voices meet on D without claiming they were ever identical.
